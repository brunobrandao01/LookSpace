# Camera Systems

The LookSpace camera architecture is designed for immersive exploration and cinematic storytelling.

## Goals

- Deliver dynamic, immersive camera systems for space and cockpit views.
- Provide cinematic camera rigs with advanced motion behavior.
- Keep camera controls responsive and production-ready.

## Architecture

- `cameras/` defines camera rig and shot management best practices.
- `cinematic/` hosts camera-driven storytelling sequences.
- `client/` integrates camera systems into the UE5 runtime.

## Key Systems

- Ship cockpit camera with HUD-linked motion.
- Orbit and planetary flyby cameras.
- Cinematic dolly, crane, and free-motion sequences.
- Camera blending and shot transition management.

## Workflow

- Define camera shot types and blend profiles.
- Use Unreal Sequencer for cinematic sequence editing.
- Validate camera behavior in multiple narrative contexts.
