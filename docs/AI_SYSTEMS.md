# AI Systems

LookSpace leverages AI systems to drive adaptive universe creation, narrative generation, and intelligent gameplay support.

## Goals

- Use AI to support content generation without sacrificing studio control.
- Enable procedural mission design with narrative coherence.
- Provide AI-assisted tooling for designers and artists.

## Components

- `ai/` orchestrates model workflows, content pipelines, and agent-based systems.
- `server/` stores AI-generated metadata and content version history.
- `universe/` uses AI output to seed simulation parameters and event triggers.

## AI Workflows

- Story and mission generation
- Procedural environment and planet variation
- Cinematic sequence planning
- Smart tooling for asset tagging and production guidance
