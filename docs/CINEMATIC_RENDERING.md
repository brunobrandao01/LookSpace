# Cinematic Rendering Pipeline

LookSpace delivers a cinematic spatial experience through carefully designed rendering pipelines and storytelling cameras.

## Pipeline Goals

- Create immersive shot composition and dynamic camera movement.
- Maintain high fidelity visuals while preserving real-time performance.
- Drive narrative pacing through lighting, effects, and atmosphere.

## Cinematic Systems

- Camera orchestration: dynamic cutscenes, flythroughs, and orbital reveals.
- Visual storytelling: timed sequences, procedural transitions, and cinematic beats.
- Rendering effects: volumetric nebula, bloom, filmic tonemapping, and depth-based effects.
- Post-processing: cinematic LUTs, digital lens artifacts, and scene grading.

## Integration

- `client/` is the primary runtime for cinematic scene playback.
- `rendering/` contains shared pipelines and shader conventions.
- `assets/` stores cinematic effect templates, camera rigs, and reference materials.
